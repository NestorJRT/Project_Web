Sustainable Development Goal 03

Nappy: small habits,
sustainable well-being.

A web application that helps students track daily sleep, hydration, physical activity and mood to make informed self-care decisions.

Project proposalGroup 6 · Nestor Ramirez and Alvaro PerezAugust 27, 2026
01 · INTRODUCTION

Health is built every day

The 2030 Agenda brings together 17 goals to improve lives and protect the planet. SDG 3 promotes healthy lives and well-being for everyone. Health is also built through daily habits: sleeping, staying hydrated, moving and recognizing our emotions.

University life makes these habits difficult to maintain. Nappy offers a simple, private tracker that turns daily data into awareness and small goals.

The app does not diagnose or replace health professionals; it supports habit-building and points users toward professional resources when needed.
02 · RATIONALE

Why did we choose SDG 3?

We chose this goal because it is close to our community, and a website can help without special equipment. Tracking a few data points reveals patterns and makes it easier to get started.

Personal relevance

Students directly face challenges involving sleep, stress and sedentary routines.

Inclusive reach

A responsive website works on phones, tablets and computers.

Technical learning

The project practices forms, data persistence, visualization and validation.

Measurable impact

We can measure registered days and completed goals.

03 · PROBLEM

Scattered information, blind decisions

Many people want to take care of themselves but do not connect their habits with their energy and mood. Existing apps can be complex or split by habit, which becomes discouraging.

Guiding question

How can we help students track and understand essential well-being habits without making self-care feel overwhelming?

Initial users

Students aged 18 to 30 who have little time but want to improve their routines.

04 · IDEA AND SCOPE

Nappy: first version

The home screen summarizes the day and lets users record each habit in a few taps. Users control their data and set gradual goals.

  • Profile and privacy.
  • Track sleep, water, exercise and mood.
  • Weekly charts and goals.
  • Responsive and accessible design.

05 · DATA AND TECHNOLOGY

A responsible, scalable solution

Data collection

Daily forms for sleep, water, activity and mood, with optional fields.

Visualization

Weekly charts, averages and streaks with accessible text.

External sources

Links to health organizations; APIs as an extension, not the main solution.

Privacy

Local or fictional data in the prototype and clear consent for accounts.

06 · EXPECTED BENEFITS

Impact for people and community

  • Self-awareness: connect habits, energy and mood.
  • Measurable goals: turn intentions into small actions.
  • Guidance: show professional resources when needed.
  • Learning: design, test and iterate an accessible website.

Prototype evaluation metrics

Completed entries, one-week return rate, achieved goals and a short usefulness and ease-of-use survey.

07 · CONCLUSION

From bold idea to useful prototype

Nappy connects SDG 3 with a concrete action: understanding our habits to better care for our health. We will begin by tracking and visualizing, then validate the idea with users and class feedback.

Next step: interview students, define the data, create wireframes and prioritize the beta version.
